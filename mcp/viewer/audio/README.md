# RobroTaxi city sound pack · elevenlabs.io

Eight sound effects generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects v3 on 22 October 2026. Each uses variant 1 from its recorded generation. The manifest includes prompts, generation IDs, file hashes and durations. The game downloads these local files only after the viewer enables Sound.

These assets were generated on the Free plan. They are for noncommercial use with ElevenLabs attribution. They are not covered by a commercial license. Before commercial distribution, replace them with appropriately licensed assets or regenerate under a qualifying paid plan. See [ElevenLabs publication guidance](https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/13313564601361-Can-I-publish-the-content-I-generate-on-the-platform) and [plan guidance](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/administration/billing).

The original downloads were stereo MP3s at 44.1 kHz. Loops received a 120 ms wrap crossfade. City details received short edge fades. All clips were normalized toward -18 LUFS with a -2 dB true-peak ceiling, then encoded as stereo MP3 at 44.1 kHz / 160 kbps. ID3 titles and the viewer credit attribute elevenlabs.io.

The city and pedestrian loops form the background. Nearby moving vehicles add electric, passenger-car and bus loops with distance, stereo position and modest speed-based pitch. Birds, a distant streetcar and a distant horn play sparingly as ambient details. These sounds do not announce simulation incidents or influence game decisions, random numbers or scores.
